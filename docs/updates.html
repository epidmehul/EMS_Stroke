Changes in simulation parameters

Configuration and data files can be provided to the simulation to change the specific distributions being used for LKW and hospital stay times as well as override other simulation parameters.

See documentation.md for more information.

Changes to default parameters/behavior

Parameter Value
Threshold for receiving EVT 6 hours
Probability of receiving IVT $$\begin{cases} 0.55 & \text{at PSC or CSC} \\ 0.4 & \text{at non-certified} \end{cases}$$
Probability of receiving EVT 0.85
Probability of early repurfusion 0.11
Risk equations for mRS Good outcome now includes mRS 2 and risk equations have been changed.
