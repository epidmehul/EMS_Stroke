Final Analytic Dataset

Accessible to CCHI and research team only · No patient name, date of birth, raw timestamps, or other direct identifiers

HIPAA Classification: Limited Data Set This dataset retains ED/hospital names and specific clinical codes (ICD-10, CPT-4, DRG) that prevent full de-identification under the Safe Harbor standard (45 CFR §164.514(b)). Access is restricted to authorized personnel in a secure computing environment.
